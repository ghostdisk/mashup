# Shared vehicles

The shared `vehicle` module defines a replaceable vehicle body (`Vehicle`), runtime velocity/yaw (`VehicleState`), and controller-neutral `DriverIntent` (throttle, steer, brake in -1..1 / 0..1). Player driving and traffic can write the same intent. `Occupancy` holds an explicit driver entity; the character remains alive and retains its inventory through a handoff. `DrivingController` binds a character and vehicle, with an enable bit for the active movement writer.

`VehicleSimulationPlugin<W>` consumes intent at `FixedUpdate` and uses the composition's `CollisionWorld::trace_aabb` for the whole vehicle body. A backend must report `supports_aabb()` or simulation holds the vehicle stationary; legacy backends do not silently pretend their standard hull is a vehicle sweep. `MeshCollisionWorld` and `FloorWorld` support the requested extents. The current simulator uses a conservative rotated world-space AABB and models an upright body without suspension or wheel contact.

Run `mashup-vehicles` for the box-car prototype. Press E near the car to enter and E again to exit; exit waits until a standing character hull has a clear placement at the driver's side. W/S accelerate/reverse, A/D steer and Space brakes. `KeyboardDrivingPlugin` is optional: compositions can omit it and provide intent from VR or AI. The same character entity is retained; the demo camera follows the vehicle while driving and the returned character on foot. This first demo uses floor-only collision and does not animate the character, provide walking controls, or model ramps/rollover. The simulator's acceleration, braking and steering are intentionally simple shared defaults, not a game-specific handling model.
